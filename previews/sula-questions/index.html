These five actually block the work

Nothing gets printed until we have these.

  1. Is it Sula or Sola? And what does it mean?

    Is it a person's name, an island or sea word, something else? And how do you say it — SOO-la, or SUH-la?

    Why it matters: the whole logo is drawn around the spelling, and the “about us” story has to be true. If this changes later, every file is redrawn.
  2. Is everything halal?

    Ingredients and process — yes or no.

    Why it matters: if yes, we can print it. If we are unsure, we cannot.
  3. Is there a deadline?

    An event, a season, a launch date you are aiming for?

    Why it matters: printing boxes takes weeks, not days. The date decides the order everything happens in.
  4. Rough budget — design, and printing?

    Just a range is fine. They are two separate numbers.

    Why it matters: it decides whether the script font is a €40 licence or a hand-lettered one, and whether the first print run is 50 boxes or 500.
  5. The label facts

    These are legally required in Canada and only you have them:

    • Producer name and physical address to print on the label
    • Exact ingredients for each product, in order — most first
    • Allergens, and whether nuts are handled in the same kitchen
    • Net weight in grams for the 4-count and the 8-count box
    • Nutrition Facts — do you have a table already, or should we build it from your recipes? (Recipe-based calculation is accepted in Canada and much cheaper than a lab test — we just need exact recipes.)
