The Story

Made by hand, by Soraya.

“

SULA SWEETS is a small, homemade bakery by Soraya Tamaddon in Surrey/Vancouver, BC. Everything is made in small batches, by hand, with an island-fresh spirit — light, playful, and made to feel like a little gift.

From pistachio, vanilla, and chocolate cookies to custom number cakes for birthdays and celebrations, each order is baked fresh and packed with care. Simple ingredients, real cooking, and a touch of the coast in every bite.

Soraya Tamaddon, founder
