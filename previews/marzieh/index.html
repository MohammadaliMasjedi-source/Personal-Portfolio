My Approach

Three practices, one path inward

Psychology, drama therapy, and mindfulness meet as one integrative practice — each offering a different doorway to the same place: a steadier, more authentic self.

Transformative Psychotherapy

Talk-based, evidence-informed work that helps you understand patterns, process difficult experiences, and move toward lasting change.

Drama Therapy

Creative, embodied methods — role play, story, and movement — that open feelings words alone can't always reach.

Mindfulness & Meditation

Guided practices that build present-moment awareness, calm the nervous system, and strengthen your relationship with yourself.
